Overview

For the renewal of a private salon’s LINE official account, the work covered information design through visual production.
The layout considers how easily guests can find information, and how the salon will keep the account running.

Who it is for

  • People who want a quiet hour at a private salon
  • People who choose a salon for its atmosphere and point of view, not only the treatment
  • First-time guests who want to check what they need before they visit

Design intent

The rich menu uses an original five-part layout. Open space keeps the salon’s unhurried feeling.
Practical information, such as booking and the menu, sits alongside the salon’s atmosphere and approach to treatment, so each purpose has a clear place.
Each visual has a role. The aim is not to pack in more, but to make the necessary information comfortable to read.
